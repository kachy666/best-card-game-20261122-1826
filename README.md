**Soup Sabotage** — a goblin chef card duel. You are Chef Pip; Baron Brine has insulted your soup in front of the whole pantry. Settle it by throwing ingredients at each other until somebody's belly gives up. 20 belly each, 3 peppers of energy a turn, 20 original ingredient cards across four flavour suits (Sweet, Spicy, Sour, Umami), and a Seasoning Chain: play two cards of the same flavour in one turn for +2. Click or use keys 1–7, Enter to play, E to end your turn. Fully offline, static files, no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8123 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8123/ in desktop Chrome. Or open `game/index.html` via any static server. `?shot=title|table|win|loss&seed=N` stages a screenshot scene.
